8 · Bitrate Sweep & Visual Comparison

H2 supported: MinRTT and ECF sustain only 38% of the ideal link’s bitrate

rtt_asym sweep (0.5–3.5 Mbit/s): MinRTT and ECF stay within 1% freeze only up to 1.0 Mbit/s, the ideal link R1 up to 2.6 (38% < 80% bar); round-robin to 2.2. F2 fails at 1.0–2.2 Mbit/s and recovers at 2.6–2.8. MinRTT, ECF, round-robin and R1 sweep points: 1 run each.

At 2.6 Mbit/s (8 runs per bar): F2, R2 and round-robin come close to the ideal link on symmetric, bw_asym and rtt_asym; MinRTT and ECF stay at 84–89%. Under outages only MinRTT and round-robin, of the multipath schedulers, keep most frames.

Usable capacity on rtt_asym: MinRTT and ECF sustain only 1000 kbps with <=1% freeze at 250 ms, compared to 2600 kbps for ideal link R1 (38%, supporting H2). The sweep points other than 2.6 Mbit/s have one run each for S1, S2, S4 and R1, so treat the exact crossing points as indicative.
