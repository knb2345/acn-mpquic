8 · Bitrate sweep on the 30/150 ms links

MinRTT and ECF stay smooth only up to 1.0 Mbit/s; an ideal link of the same capacity carries 2.6

8 paired runs per point. “Usable” means frozen for at most 1% of the time at a 250 ms deadline.

H2 holds

Usable up to: ideal link 2.6 Mbit/s, MinRTT 1.0, ECF 1.0. That is 38% of the ideal; our bar was 80% (P1, P2, Holm p = 0.020 each).

F2 beats MinRTT and ECF at 2.2 to 2.6 Mbit/s

There F2 shows 96 to 98% of frames on time, MinRTT and ECF 84 to 89%.

But not when the links are lightly used

At 1.0 to 1.6 Mbit/s F2 freezes 7 to 8% of the time. With one multipath connection, data for the fast link sat in the sender for a median 120 ms (8 runs) while that link was idle; over two separate connections (A1) it did not at 1.0 Mbit/s.

The sweep answers H2. MinRTT and ECF are fine at 1.0 Mbit/s and start freezing at 1.6. The ideal link is fine up to 2.6. F2 beats MinRTT and ECF from 2.2 Mbit/s up (round-robin was not part of the sweep), but it stalls at low load: when only the fast link has data, our single connection does not send it until something else wakes it up, about one round trip of the slow link later. A fix belongs in how the connection picks the next path to send on; we did not have time to make and re-measure it.
