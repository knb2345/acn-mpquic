1. Splitting beats pinning

Whole-frame pinning (F1) starves the second path (<24% on-time). F2's byte-split hits 98.8% on BW-asym, matching Ideal R1 (99.0%).

2. Single MPQUIC > 2 QUICs

A1 (two separate QUIC connections) collapses under load—unified congestion & stream ordering inside MPQUIC are essential.

3. Honest failure regimes

Under 2-s outages & app-limited control (4+4 Mbps), F2's 1-s max rate estimator & PTO rescue trigger premature duplicates (+17–23% bytes).

Full 2.6 Mbps results across scenarios. F2 reduces freeze fraction by ~10 percentage points on symmetric, bw_asym, and rtt_asym compared to MinRTT and ECF.
