1. F2 beats MinRTT and ECF

~10 pp less freeze on all three steady scenarios, in every pair; on bw_asym it matches the ideal link (98.8% vs 99.0%).

2. Round-robin is a strong rival

It freezes less than F2 on rtt_asym (2.1% vs 4.5%), ties on symmetric, loses on bw_asym (7.4% vs 1.3%).

3. Honest failures

Under 2-s outages F2 freezes far more than MinRTT (88% vs 34%): rescue fires too early (+16% duplicate bytes). Our F1 and A1 implementations stalled in every 2.6 Mbit/s run.

Clean data: one row per run directory under results/, run ids 1-8 (analysis/summary_final.csv). F2 reduces freeze by about 10 percentage points against MinRTT and ECF on symmetric, bw_asym and rtt_asym. Round-robin is close to F2 and better on rtt_asym. Under outages F2 is much worse. F1 and A1 stalled, so we draw no conclusion from them.
