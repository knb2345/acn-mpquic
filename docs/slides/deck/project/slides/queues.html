10 · Why MinRTT and ECF freeze

They keep 90 to 130 ms of video waiting in a link’s buffer

Equal links (40/40 ms)94 / 938 / 89 / 9
Unequal speeds (2.0 / 1.2 Mbit/s)93 / 1028 / 14210 / 11

Queue = the link’s median round-trip time during the run minus its minimum (from paths.csv, 8 runs each, 2.6 Mbit/s, after the first 5 s). ECF is within 2 ms of MinRTT everywhere.

MinRTT and ECF

They send on the lower-delay link until its window is full, and CUBIC grows the window until the 200 ms buffer is full. So 90–130 ms of video waits in line and frames miss 250 ms.

Unequal delays: round-robin wins

F2 sends more on the fast link (58% of the bytes) because the slow link takes longer to deliver, and that builds a 117 ms queue there. Round-robin gives two equal-speed links half each, so both queues stay at 9 ms.

Unequal speeds: F2 wins

Round-robin gives the 1.2 Mbit/s link 44% of the video: 0.44 × 2.6 = 1.15 Mbit/s, so that link is nearly full (142 ms queue). F2 puts 66% on the 2.0 Mbit/s link, close to its 62.5% share of capacity.

This slide explains the results with measurements, not guesses. Read the table row by row: MinRTT has long queues everywhere; round-robin is short except on the slower link when speeds differ; F2 is short except on the fast link when delays differ.
