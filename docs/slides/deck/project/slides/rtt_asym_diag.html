8 · Asymmetric RTT Path Diagnosis (30 ms vs. 150 ms)

Equal path sharing prevents head-of-line queue buildup

Deadline Resilience (H1)

At tight D = 150 ms deadlines, Round-Robin (S2) delivers 95.9% of frames on time with only 4.0% freeze, vs. 13.9% on-time (89.8% freeze) for MinRTT.

Why MinRTT & ECF Stall

Both paths carry 1.6 Mbit/s, yet MinRTT (54.7%) and ECF (54.5%) overload Path A during intra-frame bursts, inflating required buffer to 549–565 ms.

Design Takeaway

Frame-aware scheduling must respect per-path bottleneck rate rather than greedily filling the low-RTT queue.

Path-level diagnosis on rtt_asym (1.6 + 1.6 Mbit/s at 30 ms vs 150 ms RTT) explains why round-robin outperforms MinRTT and ECF. Because both paths have identical 1.6 Mbit/s capacity, sending 50.1 percent on Path A and 49.9 percent on Path B matches capacity, keeping required playout delay at 480 ms and achieving 95.9 percent on-time frames even at a strict 150 ms deadline.
