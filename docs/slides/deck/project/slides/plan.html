10 · Takeaways & Lessons Learned

What frame-aware MPQUIC teaches us for live video

1. Splitting works when the link is busy

At 2.6 Mbit/s, splitting each frame so both parts finish together (F2) is within 0.2–3.4 pp of the ideal link’s on-time ratio on steady paths and ~10 pp better than MinRTT/ECF.

2. Low bitrates and outages break it

F2 freezes at 1.0–2.2 Mbit/s; even with true link rates (R2) it fails at 1.0–1.6, so the rate estimate is not the only cause. Under outages a one-timeout rescue duplicates too much.

3. Compare with simple baselines

Plain round-robin beats MinRTT and ECF by 3–14 pp of on-time frames and rivals F2. Any new scheduler must be measured against it, not only against MinRTT.

Artifacts & Reproducibility
