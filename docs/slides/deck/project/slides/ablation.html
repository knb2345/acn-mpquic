9 · Mechanism & Hypothesis Summary

Hypotheses: what held and what did not

Path share & overhead: on bw_asym F2 sends 65.8% of bytes on the 2.0 Mbit/s path (its share of capacity: 62.5%) with 0.3% extra bytes. Under outages its rescue adds 15.5% duplicate bytes; redundant S3 adds 61–100%.

H2 (packet schedulers waste capacity): supported

MinRTT and ECF reach 38% of the ideal link’s usable capacity on rtt_asym (bar: < 80%): MinRTT fills the 30 ms path’s 200 ms buffer first. Sweep points: 1 run each.

H3 (frame splitting freezes less): partly supported

At 2.6 Mbit/s F2 freezes ~10 pp less than the better of MinRTT/ECF (P3, P4: Holm p = 0.016, 8/8 pairs). But it fails at 1.0–2.2 Mbit/s, so its usable capacity is not higher, and it is much worse under outages.

H4 (one vs two connections): not tested

Our A1 (two separate QUIC connections) stalled in every 2.6 Mbit/s run; it works only up to 1.0 Mbit/s. H1 and H5 were not run (no phone traces, no keyframe encoding).

Outcome per hypothesis, stated against the bars we fixed before any run. Supported: H2. Partly: H3. Not tested: H1, H4 (A1 stalls), H5.
